Terms of Use

The ground rules for using this website and our dispatch line.

Using this website

This website provides general information about water damage and mold. It is not legal, insurance, engineering or medical advice. Every property and every loss is different, so rely on the professionals who inspect your property and on your insurer for decisions about your claim.

Our dispatch service

Moorpark Water Damage Pros operates a 24/7 dispatch line that arranges licensed, insured crews for restoration work. Expect the plan and the price basis up front. Response is same-day when a crew has an opening, and we do not promise an arrival time.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Insurance and costs

Insurance coverage decisions belong to your insurer and your policy. Cost ranges and factors on this site are general explanations, not quotes. Your actual price is explained to you after the crew assesses the damage.

Limits

We work to keep this site accurate, but we make no promise that every page is current or without errors. Links to other sites are provided for convenience, and we are not responsible for their content.

Calls and recordings

When you call the dispatch line, you agree that the call may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Do not share payment card numbers or other sensitive financial details by phone unless a crew member on site has verified who they are.

Emergencies

If anyone is in danger, call 911. If you smell gas, see sparks or find water near live electrical equipment, leave the building and call your utility or 911 before calling us. This website is not a substitute for emergency services.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to contact@moorparkwaterdamagepros.com.
